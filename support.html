Support

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Saviour is a free rescue puzzle game by Swarjun Tech. Race the clock across five very different rescue missions — clear a path for the ambulance, free trapped wildlife, restore a patient's vital signs, find a way out of the hedge maze, and drain a flooding chamber. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach us.

Need help or want to report a problem? Email us at swarjuntech@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few business days. To help us help you, please mention your device model, OS version, and the app version (shown in Settings → About).

The rescue modes

  • Urban Rescue Grid — slide the blocking vehicles aside so the ambulance can reach the exit.
  • WildLife Rescue — place the keys by logic so no two clash, then unlock every cage.
  • Medical Lifeline — trace a single unbroken line through every point to restore the vital connection.
  • Garden Rescue — draw a route through the hedge maze to reach whoever's trapped inside.
  • Flood Rescue — rotate the pipe pieces to build an unbroken line and drain the flooding chamber.

Frequently asked questions

Is Saviour free? Why does it show ads?

Yes, Saviour is completely free to play. It's supported by ads (a banner and occasional full-screen ads between levels) delivered through Google AdMob, which keeps the game free for everyone.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account or sign-up is needed. Puzzles are generated on your device, so you can play offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the Home screen) and choose your language. Saviour is available in English, हिन्दी (Hindi) and मराठी (Marathi), and switches instantly.

How is my progress saved? Can I reset it?

Your unlocked levels and best completion times are saved locally on your device. To start fresh, open Settings → Reset progress. This clears every cleared level and best time across all rescue modes and can't be undone.

I ran out of time on a level — what are my options?

When time runs out you can watch a short ad to earn more time and keep going, or restart the level from the beginning. Every second counts!

How do I turn vibration on or off?

Toggle Vibration in Settings. This controls the haptic feedback you feel on taps and successful rescues.

How do I control the ads I see?

You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings — on iOS via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and on Android via Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Is Saviour suitable for children?

Saviour is designed for a general audience. It contains third-party ads and is not directed to children under 13. See our privacy policy for details on how data is handled.

Privacy

We built Saviour to be played without accounts or personal sign-ups. Read our full Privacy Policy to understand what limited data is involved and why.
